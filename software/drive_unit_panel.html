Steps/mm verify — command, measure, compare

Every gearset is exact by design: steps/mm = 4 × gearset teeth: 100T = 400, 80T = 320, 60T = 240, direct = 80. A small deviation diagnoses mechanics: belt tension, grub screws. A large one usually means the wrong gearset is declared in firmware: if the implied value lands on a different known gearset, this panel says so and gives you the M92 line. Writing M92 is deliberately left to the console.

This browser can’t reach the serial port

The panel talks to the board over Web Serial, which ships in desktop Chrome, Edge, and other Chromium browsers. Firefox and Safari have declined the API.

To use the panel: download this file and open it in Chrome or Edge on a desktop machine with the drive unit plugged in over USB.

On Linux, add yourself to the dialout group first, then log out and in.

Board killed (M112)

The firmware has halted everything and stopped responding. This is the intended result of KILL.

To recover: power-cycle the board or press its reset button, then reconnect. Re-home before any motion.
